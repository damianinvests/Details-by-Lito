Frequently asked questions.

Have a question about mobile car detailing in Orlando? Start here, or send a quote request with your vehicle details.

What areas does Details by Lito serve?

Details by Lito is based in the Orlando, Florida area. Mobile coverage should be confirmed for your exact city or ZIP code when you request a quote.

How much does mobile detailing cost?

Pricing can depend on vehicle size, condition, service scope, and location. Request a personalized quote rather than relying on a generic price that may not fit your vehicle.

What is the difference between detailing and paint correction?

Detailing focuses on cleaning and improving the vehicle’s overall presentation. Paint correction uses polishing to reduce the appearance of certain paint defects. Not every scratch can be safely removed, so an assessment matters.

Does ceramic coating make my paint scratch-proof?

No. Ceramic coatings can improve gloss and make routine maintenance easier, but they do not make paint scratch-proof or remove the need for careful washing.
